I have been working on something especially for small congregations with very
limited budgets, and ChurchManager is now approaching completion.

ChurchManager is a free, open-source Windows application designed to bring much
of a congregation's weekly administrative work together in one place. Its
capabilities include:

- people, family, contact, and congregation records;
- worship planning using the Historic One-Year Lectionary, Orders of Service,
  propers, hymns, sermons, participants, prayers, announcements, and preparation
  checklists;
- attendance, Communion, visitor, and follow-up records;
- private contribution records, donor statements, and fund designations;
- fund accounting, budgets, bank reconciliation, period and year-end work, and
  financial reports;
- documents, journal records, and pastoral-care planning;
- asset management for tracking the congregation's property and equipment;
- project, volunteer, and church-work scheduling;
- customizable screens and reports;
- user roles, permissions, audit history, backups, and recovery tools.

ChurchManager is not intended to replace Concordia Publishing House products,
our hymnals, or the worship and educational resources congregations already
depend upon. It is an independent administration and planning tool meant to
work alongside those resources. It stores references, selections, and planning
information rather than the full copyrighted content of published materials.

My goal is to make capable church-management software available to
congregations that may not be able to afford another subscription or a large
commercial system. There is no required purchase price or support fee. I plan
to support ChurchManager myself. Donations toward its continued development
and support will always be appreciated, but they are neither required nor
expected.

Congregations that find ChurchManager useful and wish to support its continued
development may consider a voluntary one-time donation of $50—or any amount
that fits their budget. Donations are never required.
