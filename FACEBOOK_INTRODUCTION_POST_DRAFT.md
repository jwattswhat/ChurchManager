## Full Facebook announcement

ChurchManager is now available for public beta testing.

I have been developing ChurchManager for small congregations of all
denominations, including independent and non-denominational congregations,
especially those with limited budgets. It is a free, open-source Windows
application designed to bring much of a congregation's weekly administrative
work together in one place.

ChurchManager includes:

- people, family, contact, group, and congregation records;
- worship planning with Orders of Service, lectionary references, propers,
  hymns, sermons, participants, prayers, announcements, and preparation
  checklists;
- attendance, Communion, visitor, and follow-up records;
- confidential contribution records, envelope management, contributor
  statements, and approved giving purposes;
- fund accounting, budgets, bank reconciliation, period and year-end work, and
  financial reports;
- documents, congregational journal records, pastoral-care follow-ups, assets,
  projects, events, and calendar export;
- customizable screens, menus, and reports; and
- user roles, permissions, audit history, backups, recovery tools, and support
  diagnostics.

ChurchManager is not tied to one denomination. Congregations choose and
maintain the worship resources, terminology, roles, and practices that fit
their own tradition. It is also not intended to replace denominational or
publisher products, including Concordia Publishing House products, hymnals, or
the worship and educational resources congregations already use. It is an
independent administration and planning tool designed to work alongside them.
It stores references, selections, and planning information, not the full
copyrighted content of published materials.

The public beta is an opportunity to try ChurchManager with fictional test data
or begin with a clean installation. Because this is beta software, testers may
still encounter rough edges. The website includes installation information,
testing guidance, and instructions for sending the support error log if
something goes wrong.

There is no required purchase price or support fee. I plan to support
ChurchManager myself. Congregations that find it useful and wish to support its
continued development may consider a voluntary one-time donation of $50, or
any amount that fits their budget. Donations are appreciated but never
required.
