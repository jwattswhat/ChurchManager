One practical system

The work a congregation actually needs to do

ChurchManager connects day-to-day ministry and administration while keeping sensitive areas appropriately separated.

01

People and congregation

Maintain people, families, contact details, directory privacy, attendance, and Groups.

02

Worship planning

Prepare services, Orders of Service, readings, hymns, participants, checklists, prayers, and announcements.

03

Attendance

Record members, Communion, and visitors, then use follow-up and historical attendance reports.

04

Fund accounting

Enter and post transactions, reconcile banks, manage budgets, close periods, and produce clear reports.

05

Flexible reports

Use maintained starter reports or make congregation-specific layouts with the visual designer.

06

Member giving

Maintain envelopes, reviewed contribution batches, statements, imports, corrections, and confidential donor history.

07

Groups and pastoral care

Track Group membership and attendance, assigned follow-ups, care history, and tightly protected restricted notes.

08

Church office

Catalog documents, preserve the non-financial journal, manage assets, and organize projects with due work.

09

Events and calendars

Maintain public-safe events and publish reviewed worship, Group, event, and project dates to external calendars.

10

Security and recovery

Use named users, roles, audit history, support diagnostics, protected backup and restore, and optional pastoral-note encryption.

Support ChurchManager

Free to use, with no required support fee

ChurchManager is intended especially for small congregations with very limited budgets. Donations are never required.

Congregations that find ChurchManager useful and wish to support its continued development may consider a voluntary one-time donation of $50 - or any amount that fits their budget.

Your records remain yours

A website about ChurchManager - not a home for church data

This public site has no login, congregation database, tracking, or advertising. ChurchManager itself is a desktop application, and the congregation remains responsible for its installation, backups, user access, and data.

ChurchManager supports worship planning with citations, metadata, and outlines. It is not a library of copyrighted hymn lyrics, music, liturgical wording, or Scripture text.
