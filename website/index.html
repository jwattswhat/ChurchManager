One practical system

The work a congregation actually needs to do

ChurchManager connects day-to-day ministry and administration while keeping sensitive areas appropriately separated.

01

People and congregation

Maintain people, families, contact details, directory privacy, documents, projects, and tasks.

02

Worship planning

Prepare services, Orders of Service, readings, hymns, participants, checklists, prayers, and announcements.

03

Attendance

Record members, Communion, and visitors, then use follow-up and historical attendance reports.

04

Fund accounting

Enter and post transactions, reconcile banks, manage budgets, close periods, and produce clear reports.

05

Flexible reports

Use maintained starter reports or make congregation-specific layouts with the visual designer.

06

Security and recovery

Use named users, roles, audit history, support diagnostics, and protected backup and restore.

Your records remain yours

A website about ChurchManager—not a home for church data

This public site has no login, congregation database, tracking, or advertising. ChurchManager itself is a desktop application, and the congregation remains responsible for its installation, backups, user access, and data.

ChurchManager supports worship planning with citations, metadata, and outlines. It is not a library of copyrighted hymn lyrics, music, liturgical wording, or Scripture text.
