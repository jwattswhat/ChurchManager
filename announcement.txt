Both red and white were used in ancient times (based on availability) for the celebration of Holy Communion. Theologian A. Jungmann says "when . . . the use of the purificator became general that is since the sixteenth century white wine has become commonly preferred because it leaves fewer traces in the linen." Jesus did not specify the color of the wine used for the sacrament.
